About Badia

Badia means “the desert” in Arabic. It is where we take our colors, our patience and our love of simple, well-made things.

We make custom clothes and accessories in Jordan: T-shirts, hoodies, caps and bags printed or embroidered with what you choose. A name, a team, a date, a design of your own.

Joqua is our water bottle line, made for squads, schools and everyday carry, with names and numbers on every bottle.

Every piece is Badia branded, and made for the ones you love.
